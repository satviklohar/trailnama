About us

The story behind the trail

Trailnama Adventures is a travel brand built for people who would rather be on a mountain path than in a queue. It is led by trekking creator and mountain athlete Harsh (@harshbackpack).

Our focus is simple: well-planned trips, honest pricing and memorable days outdoors.
