FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I book a trip?

Send an enquiry through the form below or on WhatsApp. We confirm availability and share the booking steps.

Do I need trekking experience?

Each trip lists a difficulty level. We have options for beginners as well as experienced trekkers.

What is included in the price?

Inclusions and exclusions are listed for every trip and confirmed before you pay.

Can you plan a trip for my group?

Yes. Tell us your dates, group size and preferences and we will plan it with you.

Can I join on my own?

Yes. Tell us when you would like to travel and we will let you know which group trips have seats.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation terms are shared with you in writing before you pay.
