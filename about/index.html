About Trailnama Adventures

A small travel brand for people who would rather be on a mountain path than in a queue.

Our story

Led by someone who lives on the trail

Trailnama Adventures is led by Harsh, a trekking creator and mountain athlete who shares trail guides, gear tests and travel stories from across India on Instagram as @harshbackpack.

Trailnama grew out of that time on the trails: trips planned by someone who walks them, with small groups, honest pricing and days paced so you can actually enjoy the view.
